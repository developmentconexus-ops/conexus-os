## 4. Protected properties and falsifiers

Challenge at least:

1. product findability, hierarchy, terminology, default routes and unnecessary depth;
2. whether a first-time internal operator can form the correct mental model without knowing operation IDs or A–O;
3. whether decision-critical identity, source, scope, currentness, consequence and next action are visible at the moment of choice;
4. whether block-local optima fail when journeys cross Workspace, Project, Control Plane and Published-App boundaries;
5. whether density and disclosure match the real task rather than the backend shape;
6. exact owner-issued coordinates, destination re-resolution and honest stale/denied/expired/dependency recovery;
7. no frontend authorization, lifecycle, durable state, join, retry, inference or semantic ownership;
8. concurrency and idempotency truth across create, approve, revise, promote, grant, decide, run and duplicate;
9. `Connection != Integration`, publication != binding, authored Agent != Release/runtime, and AVAILABLE != promoted != pointer != SERVED_VERIFIED;
10. Activity/Audit/Evidence/projection not masquerading as current owner truth;
11. exact app-scoped approval rather than universal approval authority;
12. responsive and keyboard operation, focus entry/return, overlays, text/non-color meaning and reduced motion;
13. fixture truth not masquerading as real Product truth, especially across P11 remounts;
14. no screen-shaped API, backend-shaped UX or material human need suppressed merely because current authority is inconvenient;
15. proportionality: no unnecessary ceremony, abstraction, generic registry/store or premature 4D decision.
