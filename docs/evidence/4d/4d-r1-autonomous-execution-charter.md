## 3. Part definition and review cadence

A "part" is the smallest independently green change that closes one coherent
invariant. File count is irrelevant. A0 parts are:

```text
A0-P1 = corrected A0.1
        prior-receipt freeze + migration plan + runtime/type/build probes
A0-P2 = A0.2
        canonical-json extraction + fresh G0/S1 proof
A0-P3 = A0.3 + A0.4
        named Hub composition, generator conversion, moves and strict TypeScript
A0-P4 = A0.5 + A0.6
        web strict/Biome + final import-law checker and every RED control
A0-P5 = A0.7 + corrected A0.8
        behavioral OIDC proof + full pinned qualification + receipt-last closure
```

Every part uses the same neutral review packet:

- authority and mutation scope;
- exact base/subject diff;
- commands and runtime identities;
- positive proof and RED/falsifier output;
- Product/operation/owner census delta exactly equal to the approved boundary
  contract (`0` for A0);
- known residual risks and stop conditions.

Each reviewer returns `ACCEPT`, `REVISE` or `BLOCK`, findings with severity and
falsifier, and `MATERIAL_REMAINING`. Reviewer output is Evidence; the Lead maps
it to accepted authority before changing code.

## 6. Autonomous ambiguity and stop law

Non-material choices such as local function names may be selected by consistency
and tests. Material implementation ambiguity is sent to both Fable and Gemini;
the Lead records their evidence, selects only a resolution compatible with
accepted owners, and reruns proof.

Work stops at the smallest boundary and records a morning pending item when:

- correctness requires new Product meaning, owner, Permission or trust boundary;
- a required credential/external production effect is unavailable or forbidden;
- the exact pin/advisory/source admission fails;
- predecessor evidence cannot be made fresh without mutating unrelated state;
- reviewers expose a material contradiction that cannot be resolved inside
  current authority;
- an ambiguity moves an architectural/trust boundary or requires modifying an
  approved 4F Product/slice contract; it returns to the operator for ratification;
- the same required proof remains unavailable after safe alternatives are
  exhausted.

Stopping one boundary does not authorize skipping to a later dependent boundary.
Safe independent documentation, investigation and proof-hardening may continue.
