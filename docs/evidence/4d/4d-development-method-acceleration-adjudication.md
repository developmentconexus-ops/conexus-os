## Protected claims

The amendment must preserve Product and contract authority, least-privilege and
credential custody, real PostgreSQL/Git/browser proof classes, independent
challenge for material boundaries, and clean candidate/publication custody. It
must reduce repeated execution and documentary/reviewer plumbing without making
routine mechanics a new semantic gate.

## Evidence

- Astra recommended restructuring the operational workflow while preserving the
  substantive Engineering Method; it identified recursive verification,
  dirty-tree enforcement and documentary conventions as the avoidable cost.
- Fable independently classified the needed work as local corrections with P7
  continuing. It confirmed the same duplication and retained two challengers at
  stage closure/high-risk boundaries.
- Static script expansion measured 9 Hub typechecks, 9 import-law runs and 3
  web typechecks inside one `npm run verify`; the workflow previously expanded
  those to 18, 18 and 9 respectively, with repository/wire suites repeated.
- Git history for P4–P6 contains 63 commits in a 17h41m timestamp window, 31
  documentation commits. This is fragmentation evidence, not a claim that
  documentation time equals wasted engineering time.
- P4 and P6 produced genuine authority, recovery and stale-state findings. The
  amendment therefore removes repetition around those proofs, not the proofs.

## Accepted amendment

Engineering Method 1.3 and the `conexus-development` skill now require:

1. risk-triggered independent review, with two fresh Fable + AGY/Gemini lanes
   at stage closure and material authority, trust, structural-runtime or
   external-effect boundaries;
2. a flat candidate verification graph that removes recursive duplicate classes;
   remaining intentional workflow coverage is documented and may be revisited
   only with a material coverage or cost falsifier;
3. affected checks for normal local work and a separate `verify:local` command
   for an intentionally dirty worktree;
4. clean-tree custody retained for candidate/publication gates;
5. review briefs that may be held outside the repository and adjudications that
   remain the durable Evidence;
6. no automatic three-part review quota and no automatic rerun without a
   surviving material falsifier.

The package scripts and workflow were changed only mechanically to remove
recursive duplicate checks and the early duplicate repository test. No Product
operation, contract, owner, trust boundary, provider grant or external effect
was widened.
