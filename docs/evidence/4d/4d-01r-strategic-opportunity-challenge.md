## 1. Operator correction

The operator clarified that lack of an already-instantiated consumer must not be
used to dismiss promising capabilities before evaluation. 4D must actively
reanalyze useful mechanisms and ideas that may strengthen the Product or expose
a better Global Maximum.

This corrects the inquiry posture, not the admission law:

```text
YAGNI blocks speculative adoption
!=
YAGNI blocks strategic inquiry
```

The Engineering Method already permits this distinction:

```text
authority freezes execution, not inquiry
REFERENCE_ONLY = Evidence classification
DEFER = implementation/decision disposition
```

An opportunity may therefore be studied without becoming Product authority,
dependency, SDK, runtime surface or implementation commitment.

## 2. Opportunity evaluation core

Every study must answer:

1. What Product objective, current pain, risk or defect class could this improve?
2. Does it reveal a real consumer/property that the current ledger missed?
3. Does it preserve existing semantic owners and trust boundaries?
4. Can its useful property be adopted without importing its ontology?
5. Is the behavior genuinely falsifiable at the relevant maturity level?
6. What is the operational, security, supply-chain and migration burden?
7. What is the replacement boundary and lock-in cost?
8. Can the same value be achieved by a smaller existing seam?
9. Would deferral now create structural rework or merely postpone optional value?
10. What Evidence would promote, reject or stop the opportunity?

Allowed study outcomes:

| Outcome | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `PROMOTE_TO_4D_PROPERTY` | A current Product objective/consumer or protected failure class is proved; add/revise the exact ledger row. |
| `KEEP_AS_IMPLEMENTATION_ALTERNATIVE` | The property is already admitted; retain the mechanism for 4D-C comparison. |
| `KEEP_REFERENCE_ONLY` | The source improves reasoning/proof but should not enter the Product. |
| `DEFER_WITH_TRIGGER` | Potential value is credible, but present Evidence does not justify admission; preserve the exact reopen trigger. |
| `REJECT` | The mechanism duplicates authority, contradicts the Product, or adds more total complexity than value. |
| `STOP` | A required property cannot be decided safely without a prerequisite or real-dependency probe. |

Popularity, novelty and demo quality are discovery signals only.

- Stop when further sources stop changing the decision space.
- Never install a candidate merely to make the research feel concrete.
- A targeted isolated probe requires its own explicit proof question and must
  not become Product implementation.

## 8. Operator-directed adversarial certainty protocol

During 4D planning, a material unresolved claim may pause the current Wave and
open the smallest bounded proof layer required for certainty:

```text
owner/index/decision reconstruction
→ current primary documentation/source
→ isolated deterministic probe where mechanics are deciding
→ real dependency/model/browser/database/provider proof only for its named claim
→ independent whole-package challenge when authority or Global Maximum is material
→ Lead adjudication back into the smallest owner
```

Subagents may parallelize independent research/proof questions; they do not own
adjudication. Claude Code/Fable and Gemini may be used as external challengers
when available and authenticated. Tool/model unavailability is reported, never
simulated. A probe remains Evidence-only, uses exact pins/provenance, records its
limitations and cannot install or implement Product code by implication.
