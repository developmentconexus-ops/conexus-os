### Proof-custody and consumer-eligibility rule

Every locally consumed subject requires a named producer, an exact
reproducible command plus version/configuration, immutable candidate/tuple/config
identity, canonical subject bytes or a digest of those bytes, explicit coverage
and observed falsifiers, a current owner disposition, and the consuming package
explicitly named as eligible. Missing any field means `NOT ELIGIBLE`; it is
never inferred from a matching digest, a `PASS` label, a fixture, queue state,
a transient adopter, a historical receipt or a “routed” dependency.

Upstream P2–P5 local producers may emit and consume only their own eligible
scoped subjects and must not consume future P6. P6 consumes those independent
inputs and is the producer of the sole current aggregate receipt; downstream
aggregate consumers wait for that P6 output.

This is proof-custody/consumer-eligibility documentation only. It does not
define Product meaning, create a new operation, table or authority, execute
P6, or establish current proof.

## Review routing

Review is proportional to risk and grouped by a verifiable candidate:

- routine mechanical work: targeted proof and Lead review;
- new owner, trust boundary, schema/runtime structure or irreversible effect:
  material checkpoint before dependent work consumes it;
- R3 closure: candidate-wide proof and two fresh independent lanes unless an
  exact operator waiver applies;
- post-review correction: repeat only when the protected property or proof
  reliability changed materially.

The current R3 operator waiver is limited to its recorded checkpoint. It does
not claim independent closure or waive future material stage closure.
